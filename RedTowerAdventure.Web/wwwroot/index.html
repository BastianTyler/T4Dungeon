// CORE SYSTEMS

Everything a dungeon needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

[#]
PROCEDURAL MAPS

Every run generates a new 10×10 grid of combat, treasure, shops, and exits. Fog of war keeps the unknown dangerous.

[!]
REAL-TIME COMBAT

Four distinct minigame types — Timed Press, Mash, Sweet Spot, Sequence. React fast or take the hit.

[$]
MERCHANT SYSTEM

Spend hard-earned gold at roving caravans. Upgrade gear, stock potions, prepare for the next floor.

[+]
INVENTORY & GEAR

Equip weapons and armor across multiple slots. Use consumables in and out of combat. Every item has weight.

[*]
LOOT SYSTEM

Enemies drop gold and items on randomized loot tables. Rare drops exist. Fortune favors the persistent.

[>]
PURE C# / TERMINAL

No engine, no dependencies, no bloat. Runs anywhere .NET runs. Your CPU does the work.

// COMBAT ENGINE

Fight with your
reflexes.

When enemies strike, the game doesn't pause. You have seconds to counter. Each enemy move triggers a different minigame — master all four or get wrecked.

  • Timed Press — hit the key before the bar fills
  • Key Mash — spam fast enough to reach the goal
  • Sweet Spot — stop the marker inside the green zone
  • Sequence — press keys in rapid succession without missing

// WORLD GENERATION

Every floor.
Different.

The dungeon generates fresh every run. Cells hide behind fog of war until explored. Find the exit or die trying — treasures, merchants, and monsters fill the space between.
